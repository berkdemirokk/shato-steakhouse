# Smoke & Ember

A warm, cinematic dark theme inspired by premium steakhouses (Hawksmoor London, COTE NYC, Carbone). Designed for restaurants where atmosphere, fire, and craft meet — meat-forward, slow, expensive, confident.

## Color Palette

### Background
- **Warm Void**: `#0F0B08` — Deepest background, almost black with warm hue
- **Cocoa**: `#1A1410` — Surface (sections, cards on hover)
- **Bark**: `#241B16` — Elevated surfaces (form fields, modals)
- **Ember Glow**: `rgba(194, 105, 63, 0.06)` — Subtle warm wash for accents

### Text
- **Vanilla Cream**: `#EDE4D3` — Primary text (headings, body)
- **Light Smoke**: `#B8AC98` — Secondary text (descriptions, lead)
- **Smoke**: `#7A6E5C` — Muted (small caps, labels)
- **Ash**: `#4A4036` — Subtle (dividers, deprecated)

### Accents
- **Ember**: `#C2693F` — Primary accent (CTAs, links, dividers)
- **Flame**: `#D17847` — Hover state for primary
- **Ember Deep**: `#9C5230` — Active/pressed
- **Brass**: `#B8895A` — Secondary accent (badges, italic emphasis)
- **Brass Light**: `#D4AC7E` — Highlight (gold details, stars)

### Borders & Lines
- **Whisper**: `rgba(237, 228, 211, 0.08)` — Most borders
- **Edge**: `rgba(237, 228, 211, 0.15)` — Defined borders
- **Ember Line**: `rgba(194, 105, 63, 0.3)` — Accent borders

## Typography

- **Display (H1, H2, Hero)**: `Fraunces` — Variable serif with optical sizing. Italic for emphasis. Use weights 300, 400, 600, 900.
- **Body**: `Inter` — Clean, legible sans. Weights 300 (lead), 400 (body), 500 (UI), 600 (buttons).
- **Mono accent**: `JetBrains Mono` — For small uppercase labels, metadata, eyebrow text.

### Type Scale
- Hero: clamp(56px, 9vw, 128px)
- H2: clamp(40px, 5vw, 72px)
- H3: 32px
- H4: 24px
- Lead: 20px / 1.7
- Body: 16px / 1.7
- Small: 14px
- Eyebrow (label): 12px uppercase, 2px letter-spacing

## Spacing Scale
- xs: 8px · sm: 16px · md: 24px · lg: 48px · xl: 96px · 2xl: 160px

## Design Principles
- Generous whitespace (sections 120-160px padding)
- No rounded corners on display elements (2-4px max)
- Single 1px hairline borders, never heavy
- Real photography over gradients
- Subtle film grain texture overlay on hero
- Animations: slow ease-out (cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)), 600-800ms
- Hover: warm overlay + 1.02 scale on images, ember underline on links

## Best Used For
Premium restaurants, whisky bars, steakhouses, fine dining, butcher shops, craft cocktail venues.
